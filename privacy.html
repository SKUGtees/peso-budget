Privacy Policy - Budget Abroad

Last updated: July 2026

Budget Abroad is a personal budgeting app. This page explains what data the app handles and how.

Your budget data stays on your device

Everything you enter - income, spending entries, savings goals, monthly costs, and settings - is stored only in your device's local app storage. It is never transmitted to, or stored on, any server operated by us. We have no accounts, no login, and no way to see your financial data. If you uninstall the app or clear its storage, that data is permanently gone unless you've used the in-app "Export backup" feature to save a copy yourself.

Daily reminder notifications

If you turn on the optional daily reminder, your device registers for push notifications. To schedule the reminder at your chosen time, a small amount of technical data - your notification subscription details and the time you picked - is relayed through a third-party message-relay service (ntfy.sh) and read by an automated scheduler we run. This data is used solely to decide when to send your reminder and is not used for any other purpose, sold, or shared.

Advertising

Budget Abroad shows ads served by Google AdMob. Google may collect certain data (such as advertising identifiers and device information) to serve and measure ads, in accordance with its own privacy policy. See Google's Advertising Policy and Google Privacy Policy for details on what Google collects and how it's used.

Children's privacy

Budget Abroad is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
