What currency do you get paid in?

If you earn money in Euros, US Dollars, or Canadian Dollars but spend Mexican Pesos day to day, pick your currency below — we'll convert it to pesos automatically using live exchange rates.

If you're paid directly in Mexican Pesos, pick Mexican Peso (MXN) — the app skips all conversion and just becomes a simple peso savings tracker.
